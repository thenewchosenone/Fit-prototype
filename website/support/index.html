Lift Rivals Support

How can we help?

Get help with your account, training data, privacy requests, lift reports, or technical issues.

Contact support

Email support@liftrivals.com. Include the email address connected to your Lift Rivals account and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment information.

Email Lift Rivals Support

Account deletion

In the Lift Rivals app, open Settings, choose Delete Account, and confirm. If you cannot access the app, email support from the address connected to your account and request deletion.

Privacy and safety

For privacy requests, account access concerns, or reports about content or another user, contact support using the email address above.

Lift Rivals is not medical advice. Stop exercising if you feel pain or unsafe and seek help from a qualified health professional when appropriate.

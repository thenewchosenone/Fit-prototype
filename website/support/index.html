Lift Rivals Support

Account, training, privacy, and launch support

Contact support

For help with your account, a workout, privacy request, or report, email support@liftrivals.com. Include the email on your Lift Rivals account and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment information.

Account deletion

To delete your account, open Lift Rivals, go to Settings, choose Delete Account, and confirm. If you cannot access the app, contact support from the account email and request deletion.

Safety

Lift Rivals is not medical advice. Stop an exercise if you feel pain or unsafe and seek qualified help for health concerns.

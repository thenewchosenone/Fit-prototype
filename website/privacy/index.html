Privacy Notice

Effective August 6, 2026

This notice explains how Lift Rivals collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Lift Rivals app, website, and support services.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide, including your email address, account identifiers, profile name, profile photo, biography, gym affiliations, friendships, workout and lift records, uploaded photos or videos, reports, and support messages. Sign in with Apple may provide an Apple account identifier, name, and relay or regular email address according to your Apple choices.

We also process limited technical and usage information needed to operate and secure the service, such as device identifiers, app interactions, coarse location when supplied by the platform or network, diagnostics, authentication events, and timestamps.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, synchronize training history, calculate rankings and achievements, provide social features, display content according to its visibility setting, investigate reports, enforce community standards, prevent fraud or abuse, respond to support requests, and improve reliability.

3. Public and community content

Profile details, gym memberships, rankings, public lift submissions, photos, and videos may be visible to other users when you choose or use a public feature. Private or local-only training data is not intentionally published. Do not upload sensitive information that you do not want others to see.

4. Sharing and providers

We share information only as needed to operate the service, comply with law, protect users, or complete a transaction you request. Infrastructure and authentication providers, including Supabase and Apple, process information on our behalf or under their own terms. We do not sell personal information and do not use third-party advertising trackers.

5. Moderation and safety

Authorized reviewers may access reported content and related account information to investigate safety, integrity, or policy concerns. We may preserve or disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, enforce our terms, or protect users and the public.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain account information while your account is active and as needed for the purposes described above. Some records may be kept longer for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. You can request permanent account deletion from Settings in the app. Deletion removes or de-identifies account data subject to limited lawful retention.

7. Your choices

You can edit profile information, choose content visibility, block users, report content, revoke photo access in device settings, and delete your account in the app. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information.

8. Security and processing

We use administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be processed in the United States or other locations where our providers operate, subject to applicable protections.

9. Children

Lift Rivals is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Changes

We may update this notice as the service changes. We will post the revised effective date and provide additional notice when required.

Questions or privacy requests

Use the Lift Rivals Support page to contact us about this notice or a privacy request.

Privacy Notice

Effective July 24, 2026

This notice explains how LiftRank collects, uses, and protects information when you use the LiftRank iOS app and related services.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as email address, username, display name, and profile photo.
  • Profile details such as age group, division, experience, height, bodyweight, unit preference, and city.
  • Training data such as exercises, sets, reps, weights, workouts, personal records, bodyweight logs, plans, and progress.
  • Community content you choose to share, including posts, messages, reports, shared workouts, and lift media.
  • Technical information needed to operate the service, protect accounts, deliver notifications, and diagnose failures.

How we use information

We use information to provide the app, sync your account, calculate progress and rankings, provide community features, verify eligible lifts, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, and improve reliability. We do not use advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking for advertising.

Sharing and visibility

Information is shared with service providers that host and operate LiftRank, including our database, storage, authentication, and notification infrastructure. Community and ranking information is visible according to your privacy controls and the feature you use. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

We retain information while your account is active and as needed for security, legal, dispute, and audit purposes. You can request account deletion from Settings in the app. Account deletion removes the account and associated personal data subject to limited lawful retention.

Your choices

You can update profile information and privacy controls in the app, control what you share, and contact support for access or correction requests. For help, visit Support.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through our support page.

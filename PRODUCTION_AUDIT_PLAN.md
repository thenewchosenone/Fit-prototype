**Deliverable:** a security/data-flow map, reproducible findings with relevant SQL/runtime evidence, and verified recovery and conflict behavior.

## Pass 2 — Prove the product works

**Scope:** end-to-end functionality; workout lifecycle and recovery; lift/PR calculations and ranking rules; forum and moderation; App Store submission paths.
